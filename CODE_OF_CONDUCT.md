# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SIGNAL and its community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient, and specific—especially with first-time contributors.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's contact details or private data.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and learn from the review.
- Put the community and the safety of SIGNAL users ahead of individual credit.

Harassment, insults, sexualized attention, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the MU Enigma organizing team through an official committee channel. Organizers may remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
